## Product and editorial decisions

- Preserve the original hero heading and supporting paragraph verbatim, together
  with Nimbus Sans, glass surfaces, brand marks, pills and the three scene styles.
- Position around **Cross-border settlement infrastructure for India**, including
  discussions in both directions. Do not narrow the marketing positioning using
  an internal implementation document.
- The discovery guide is a scoping tool. Its six currency choices are proposals
  to discuss, not a statement of live coverage or a price quote.
- Visitor selections change the direction, funding/destination, purpose evidence
  and generated downloadable brief. A separate context field accompanies the
  inquiry, preserving the visitor's own message.
- Give PSPs, merchants/platforms, OTC/liquidity teams, payment operators and
  institutional partners distinct three-step business workflows.
- Explain beneficiary information, purpose/documents, execution terms and records
  concretely. Do not invent regulatory approvals, partner logos, customer names,
  traffic/volume figures, savings, service levels or certificates.
- Remove decorative side labels and pedestal microcopy. Clean generated facade
  lettering while preserving the architectural composition.
- Animate tapered light trails along the gold and mint ribbon paths in canvas over the existing hero artwork. Keep the emblem and scene static.
  Honour pause, reduced motion, inactive tabs and an offscreen hero.
